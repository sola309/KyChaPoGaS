Across every street and rooftop in the frame, pale wraiths stand scattered - and on the first

indifferent.

the thing above them all is never once struck, never once aimed at. Full force, spent entirely
on the wrong target. The picture holds one beat on the untouched sky over the seething ground.

almost still except for her small receding figure. Then, at the far edge of hearing distance,

them; the frame does not decide). She does not wave and they do not wave. A breath. She turns

every sound gone. {{S:STORM}} {{LENS}} {{FOCUS}} {{STYLE}}

her far footsteps, which stop once, resume, and thin into silence. By the end, nothing.
